# CRAVE Core 5 Screen Mockups

Status date: September 7, 2026  
Status: Visual direction draft, not implementation-ready  
Screens: Home, Search, Results, Map, Settings

## Direction

This pass intentionally steps away from dense system-board language and focuses on five clean app screens.

The style target:

- warmer and more premium;
- less dashboard-like;
- less badge-heavy;
- more food/photo-led;
- clearer primary actions;
- enough intelligence to feel like CRAVE, not enough annotation to feel like an internal spec.

## Screens

1. Home — appetite-forward start, quick craving prompt, nearby/live context.
2. Search — calmer natural-language entry with useful shortcuts.
3. Results — one strong answer, two alternatives, plain reason copy.
4. Map — Search-owned candidates spatialized with a selected place card.
5. Settings — account, location, dietary, offline, privacy controls.

## Brutal Self-Grade

8.2/10 direction.

This is much more usable as a mockup board than the 16-state wall. It feels closer to something a founder/designer can react to quickly.

What still needs work:

- Real food photography will make or break it.
- The Home screen could become more distinctive.
- Settings is intentionally simple but may be too generic.
- Results still needs one more typography pass to feel truly premium.
